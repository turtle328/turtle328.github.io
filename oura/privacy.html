Privacy Policy

Tasker Oura Scores is a personal-use application.

The application accesses Oura daily summary data solely for the purpose of personal automation.

Selected daily scores may be sent by SMS to a recipient explicitly configured by the application owner.

Oura data is not sold, used for advertising, or otherwise shared with third parties except as necessary to perform that user-configured SMS automation.

Data retrieved from Oura is processed only as necessary to perform the personal automation.
